1. Core Loan Summary / Overview Dashboard (First screen they see)
* Loan account number / reference ID (masked or partial for security)
* Loan type / product name (e.g., Personal Loan, Auto Loan, Mortgage, Business Line of Credit)
* Original loan amount
* Current outstanding principal balance
* Current total balance owed (principal + interest + fees)
* Current interest rate (fixed or variable; show effective rate if variable)
* Loan start/origination date
* Loan maturity / final payment date
* Loan status (e.g., Current, Delinquent, In Grace Period, In Forbearance, Paid Off, Defaulted)
* Overall progress bar or percentage paid off (e.g., "65% complete")

2. Payment & Repayment Details
* Next due date and amount due (principal + interest + fees)
* Payment frequency (monthly, bi-weekly, etc.)
* Payment method on file (Mpesa or bank deposit) — with option to update
* Last payment date, amount, and how it was applied (principal vs. interest vs. fees)
* Payment history table (last 12–24 months or full history):
    * Date paid
    * Amount paid
    * Status (On time, Late, Partial, Returned/NSF)
    * Breakdown (principal, interest, fees)
* Remaining payments / amortization schedule preview (e.g., next 6–12 months)
* Early payoff / payoff quote (current exact amount to pay off today, including any prepayment penalties)
* Late fee / penalty structure (if applicable)
* Grace period details
3. Interest & Fee Breakdown
* Current accrued interest
* Lifetime interest paid to date
* Estimated total interest over life of loan (vs. actual if variable)
* Any fees charged (origination, late, NSF, etc.) with dates and amounts
4. Documents & Statements
* Secure access to downloadable/viewable documents:
    * Original loan agreement 
    * Most recent monthly statement
    * Payment receipts / confirmation
    * Amortization schedule (full or printable)
5. Account Management & Actions
* Make a payment (one-time or scheduled) — with amount selector (minimum, full, custom)
* Change payment due date (if allowed by product)
* Request payment holiday / deferment / forbearance (with eligibility check)
* Update personal/contact info (phone, email, address)
* Upload documents (e.g., proof of income for modification request)
* View/download transaction history (PDF/CSV export)
6. Notifications & Alerts Section
* In-portal message center or alert banner:
    * Upcoming payment reminders
    * Late payment warnings
    * Rate change notices (for variable loans)
    * Important account updates (e.g., escrow analysis)
* Option to manage notification preferences (email, SMS)
