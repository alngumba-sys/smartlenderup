THE FIX:
    Updated vite.config.ts to exclude packages that use WebAssembly.
    This prevents Vite from trying to pre-bundle them.


WHY IT WORKS:
    @supabase/supabase-js and xlsx use WASM internally.
    Vite was failing to compile them.
    Excluding them = No compilation = No error.
